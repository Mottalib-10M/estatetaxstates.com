2025 Form CT-706 NT Instructions
Connecticut Estate Tax Return (for Nontaxable Estates)
General Information
For decedents dying during 2025, the Connecticut estate tax
exemption amount is  $13.99 million . Therefore, Connecticut
estate tax is due from a decedent’s estate if the Connecticut taxable
estate is more than $13.99 million.
