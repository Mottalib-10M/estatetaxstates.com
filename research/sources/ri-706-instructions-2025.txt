Titre: RI-706 Estate Tax Return Instructions (2025) - State of Rhode Island Division of Taxation

[Part 4 - Computation of Tax]
Line 2 - Total gross estate less exclusion from page 4, Part 6, line 12. Line 3 - Rhode Island Gross Estate For a decedent domiciled in Rhode Island: Enter the amount of Rhode Island Assets excluding non-Rhode Island real and tangible property. For a decedent non-domiciled in Rhode Island: Enter the amount of Rhode Island real and tangible property. Line 4 – Determine the percentage of which the Rhode Island gross estate is of the federal gross estate. Divide line 3 by line 2. Carry to four decimal places.
