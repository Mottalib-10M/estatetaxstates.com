IA 706 Iowa Inheritance Tax Return Instructions 
revenue.iowa.gov 
60-066a (07/11/2025) 
Who Must File? 
Inheritance tax has been repealed for estates of 
individuals with a date of death on or after 
January 1, 2025. An Iowa inheritance tax return 
must be filed for an estate when the gross share, 
subject to tax without reduction for liabilities, of 
any beneficiary, heir, transferee, or surviving joint 
tenant exceeds the allowable exemption from 
such share or if a federal return has been filed. 
(See below for deaths on or after July 1, 2004 but 
before January 1, 2025.) The term estate includes 
property held in trust or otherwise not subject to 
probate.
