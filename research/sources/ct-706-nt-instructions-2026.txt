2026 Form CT-706 NT Instructions
Connecticut Estate Tax Return (for Nontaxable Estates)
General Information
For decedents dying during 2026, the Connecticut estate tax
exemption amount is $15 million . Therefore, Connecticut estate
tax is due from a decedent’s estate if the Connecticut taxable estate
is more than $15 million.
